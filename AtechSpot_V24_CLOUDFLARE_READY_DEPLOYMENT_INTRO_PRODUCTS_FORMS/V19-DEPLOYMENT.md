## Cloudflare Pages deployment

1. Extract the ZIP before uploading.
2. Upload all extracted files and folders together to the existing `atechspot` Pages project.
3. Do not disconnect `atechspot.com` or `www.atechspot.com`.
4. Open the new unique `pages.dev` deployment URL first.
5. Test `/`, `/services`, `/business`, `/learning`, `/resources`, `/shop`, `/careers`, `/intake`, `/contact`, `/booking`, `/payment`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/accessibility`.
6. Submit test requests through contact and intake, then confirm delivery to `aplustechucation@gmail.com`.
7. After verification, open `https://www.atechspot.com` and hard-refresh.

## Important

Upload the extracted contents, not the ZIP file itself. The root of the upload must contain `index.html`, `_headers`, `_redirects`, `assets`, `functions`, and the other site files.
