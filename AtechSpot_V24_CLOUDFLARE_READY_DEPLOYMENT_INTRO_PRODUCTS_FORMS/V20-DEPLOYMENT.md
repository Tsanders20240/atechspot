# A+ Techucation V20 — Cinematic Revenue + Remote Support

## Design direction

V20 uses large, premium technology photography, bold editorial headlines, generous spacing and focused calls to action. It combines image-led technology storytelling with a symptom-first support journey without copying another company’s protected branding, claims, warranties or language.

## Major V20 additions

- Cinematic homepage hero with original A+ Techucation technology imagery
- Large remote-support feature panel and dedicated support page
- Permission-based remote-access process and scam-safety language
- Business automation and ecommerce image-led revenue feature
- Outcome-based Technology Guide
- Common-needs service router for slow computers, data loss, weak website conversion and repetitive work
- V19 revenue paths, mobile actions, analytics and all prior features retained

## Preserved from V18/V19

- All HTML routes and clean-URL copies
- Services, business, learning, resources, shop, careers and internship content
- Intake, contact, booking, payment and data-recovery workflows
- Cloudflare functions, redirects, headers, sitemap and robots configuration
- Privacy, terms, accessibility and affiliate disclosures
- Analytics, chat, Messenger, DriveSavers disclosures and downloadable learning PDF

## Deployment

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Upload all extracted files and folders to the existing Cloudflare Pages `atechspot` project.
3. Open the unique deployment URL first and test the pages listed below.
4. Do not disconnect `atechspot.com` or `www.atechspot.com`.
