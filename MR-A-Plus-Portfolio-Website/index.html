A good portfolio should not end with “look at my work.” It should help the right prospect identify fit and move into the next action.

HOW THE JOURNEY WORKS
1Assess: define the business problem, current systems and desired result.
2Scope: identify the smallest useful implementation and what should be automated or delegated.
3Build: create the agreed system with testing and clear ownership.
4Launch: deploy, verify customer paths and connect analytics/operations where applicable.
5Optimize: improve based on real usage, revenue and operational evidence.
