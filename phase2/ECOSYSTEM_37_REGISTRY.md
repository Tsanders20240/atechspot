## Production alignment rule

A destination is not considered 10/10 until:
- hostname/path resolves

- correct application/brand renders
- primary conversion works
- analytics and error logging work
- mobile/accessibility/security QA pass
- rollback/backup procedure exists
- no critical launch-gate issue remains
