HOW PAID SUPPORT WORKS

A clear path from issue to resolution.

A request starts a conversation; it does not purchase a session or grant access.

Describe the issue

Tell us the device, symptoms, what you tried and the result you need. Do not send credentials or private files.

Approve scope and price

We confirm the proposed work, expected fee and limitations in writing. You decide whether to proceed.

Pay through an approved link

After approval, use the Stripe invoice or payment link we provide. Never put card details in the support form.

Authorize the session

We provide connection instructions only when needed. You approve the connection and can end it at any time.

Review the result

We explain what was done, what remains unresolved and the next practical step or referral.

Get follow-up help

Use the support contact route for questions about the agreed work, a receipt or a new issue.

BEFORE YOU REQUEST

Common questions.

How much does it cost?

There is no charge to send a request. The support fee depends on the issue and scope. We confirm the price before paid work.

Do I need the general consultation?

No. Describe the issue above first. We recommend support, a specialist or another step based on the details.

How do you connect remotely?

If remote access is appropriate, we send approved instructions after scope and price are agreed. Do not grant access to an unexpected caller.

What if it cannot be fixed remotely?

We explain apparent limitations before starting and document any remaining issue or referral after the authorized work.
