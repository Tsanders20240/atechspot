Careers & Internship Program

A structured, mentor-led technology and e-commerce learning experience for college students seeking practical skills and, when approved by their school, academic credit.

Apply for an InternshipReview the Program
Program design

Learning comes first.

Projects are tied to written learning objectives, supervised by a designated mentor, and evaluated through regular feedback.

Learning plan

Defined objectives

Each intern receives a skills plan covering technology support, website operations, AI workflows, digital accessibility, e-commerce, or content systems.

Mentorship

Supervised experience

A mentor assigns educational projects, reviews work, and completes midterm and final evaluations when required by the school.

Academic credit

School coordination

Credit is awarded only by the student’s college or university. The student must obtain approval and provide any required school agreement before starting.

Documentation

Hours and evaluations

Weekly time logs, progress reports, learning reflections, and completion records support school verification.

Houston

Local and remote options

Eligible placements may be remote, hybrid, or Houston-based depending on the project, supervision needs, and school requirements.

Paid-first approach

Compensation reviewed

A+ Techucation reviews compensation and worker classification before placement. Academic credit alone does not make an unpaid internship lawful.

Possible learning tracks

Build career-ready experience.

  • Website content and quality assurance
  • AI prompt systems and responsible automation
  • Digital accessibility support
  • E-commerce listing, inventory, and analytics
  • STEM education and learning content
  • Customer intake and business operations

Before placement

  • School approval or advisor contact, when seeking credit
  • Written learning objectives
  • Defined dates, schedule, mentor, and project scope
  • Compensation and classification review
  • Signed confidentiality and technology-use documents
Internship application

Tell us about your program and goals.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, government IDs, passwords, financial account information, or medical information.

Important: Internship structure, compensation, school credit, and work authorization are reviewed before any placement begins. Students seeking credit must coordinate with their academic department. International students must obtain any required authorization from their school before beginning work.
