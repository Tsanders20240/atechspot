Privacy & Child Safety

No child account is required. The site does not ask a child for a name, email, birthday, school, address or public profile. A–Z activities run in the browser. Adult institutional inquiries use the separate secure ATechSpot contact system.

Terms

ABC's of Technology provides educational content, games and printable activities. Book, audiobook and media transactions occur only on third-party platforms. Their pricing, taxes, shipping, subscriptions, returns and account rules control those transactions.

Accessibility

Core navigation is keyboard accessible, focus remains visible, text uses strong contrast, motion respects reduced-motion preferences, images include alternate text and activities do not depend on audio alone.
