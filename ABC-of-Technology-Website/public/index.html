TRUST + SAFETY

Built for kids to explore and grown-ups to control.

Privacy & Child Safety

No child account is required. This site does not ask a child for a name, email, birthday, school, address or public profile. The A–Z activities run in the browser and do not need a login. Adult institutional inquiries go to the separate ATechSpot secure contact system. External retailers and media platforms have their own privacy practices.

Terms

ABC's of Technology provides educational content, games and print-ready activities. Book, audiobook and media transactions occur only on third-party platforms. Their pricing, taxes, shipping, subscriptions, returns and account rules control those transactions. Site content and original characters remain protected intellectual property.

Accessibility

Core navigation is keyboard accessible, focus styles are visible, motion respects reduced-motion preferences, text maintains strong contrast, and activities do not depend on audio alone. Browser print tools provide a practical way to enlarge or save printable activity pages.
