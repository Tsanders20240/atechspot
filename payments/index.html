Payments & Billing

Clear pricing, secure Stripe-hosted checkout and written scope before custom implementation.

Who charges you

ATechSpot is operated by A+ Techucation LLC. Payments for ATechSpot services are charged by A+ Techucation LLC through Stripe. Stripe securely processes payment-card and eligible bank-payment information. ATechSpot does not ask you to send full card numbers, bank passwords or authentication codes through website forms, email or chat.

Executive Growth Review — $997 one time

The Executive Growth Review is a one-time professional service. The checkout page shows the amount before payment. Payment does not create an open-ended development engagement; ATechSpot confirms scheduling, requested inputs and the review scope after payment. If you need to cancel before substantive review work begins, contact Billing promptly. Once substantive work has begun or third-party costs have been committed, amounts already earned or committed may be non-refundable, subject to applicable law.

Project Builds — from $2,500

Custom websites, apps, ecommerce, automation and software projects are not sold as undefined one-click purchases. ATechSpot first evaluates the project, then confirms scope, deliverables, schedule, dependencies, third-party costs, milestones and payment terms in writing. Any project deposit or milestone invoice is governed by the accepted proposal or service agreement.

GrowthCare — from $750/month

GrowthCare is recurring professional support and optimization. It is activated only after ATechSpot confirms the service scope in writing. The agreed recurring charge renews for each billing period until canceled under the applicable service terms. Canceling stops future renewals; service normally continues through the already-paid billing period unless the written agreement states otherwise. No cancellation fee is charged by ATechSpot for stopping future renewals.

Recurring-payment consent

Before a recurring payment is submitted, the checkout experience identifies the recurring amount, billing frequency and that the service renews until canceled. Do not complete checkout unless you agree to those terms. ATechSpot provides a straightforward billing-support route for cancellation and will add Stripe self-service billing access wherever supported.

Refunds, billing errors and disputes

Refund eligibility depends on the purchased service, whether work has begun, any accepted proposal, and applicable law. Duplicate charges, unauthorized charges and billing errors should be reported promptly through Billing. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived. A charge dispute does not replace the project or membership cancellation process, and contacting Billing first often allows the issue to be resolved faster.

Taxes and third-party costs

Applicable taxes and approved third-party costs may be added when required. Custom proposals identify material third-party costs when known. Tax treatment can vary by customer location and service; ATechSpot may use Stripe or other providers to calculate or collect taxes where configured and required.

Payment security

Use only payment links reached from ATechSpot.com, A+ Techucation, a written ATechSpot proposal/invoice, or an ATechSpot message you expected. ATechSpot will never ask you to email a full payment-card number, CVV, online-banking password or one-time authentication code.

Billing help

For payment, invoice, cancellation or refund questions, use the secure Billing contact route. For legal or policy questions, use the Legal contact route.
