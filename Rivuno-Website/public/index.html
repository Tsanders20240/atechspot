Questions before launch.

Can I buy ABC of Tech on Rivuno today?

Not yet. The planned permanent digital download price is $1.29, but Rivuno is not accepting payment until protected delivery and account-linked purchase records are connected.

Can I listen to the release now?

Yes. The featured release links directly to its current Amazon Music and YouTube Music destinations.

Why does Rivuno separate master and composition rights?

They are distinct rights records. Rivuno is being designed to store and account for them separately instead of treating a release as a single undifferentiated rights object.

Can other artists upload music yet?

Not yet. Third-party onboarding will open only after seller agreements, rights intake, payment, tax, refund, reporting, payout, and dispute workflows are ready.
