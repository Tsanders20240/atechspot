FIT BEFORE BUILD

Custom when custom creates real value.

Not every business needs custom software. We compare the workflow, cost, risk and long-term maintenance requirements before recommending a build.

When a custom app can make sense

  • Your process is unique enough that generic tools create constant workarounds.
  • Multiple systems need a simpler interface or connected workflow.
  • Customers need a branded self-service experience.
  • Manual work is creating delay, errors or poor visibility.
  • The app supports a defined revenue, service or operational objective.

When we may recommend something simpler

  • A configured existing platform can solve the problem reliably.
  • The workflow is not stable enough to automate yet.
  • The expected business value does not justify custom development.
  • A website, form or automation can deliver the same outcome faster.
  • Maintenance requirements would create unnecessary complexity.
