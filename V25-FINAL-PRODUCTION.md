# ATechSpot V25 — final cinematic Apple-style production deployment

This is the complete production package to deploy to `www.atechspot.com`.

## Included

- Full-width privacy-enhanced YouTube introduction using video `1MDUmj0Pwss`.
- Film starts at 30 seconds, autoplays muted, loops and is presented in black and white.
- A separate `Watch With Sound` action opens the source video at 30 seconds.
- Mr. A+ Portfolio buttons in the introduction, home hero, global navigation and page funnels.
- Apple-inspired typography, spacing, monochrome panels and focused calls to action.
- Digital Products and Small Business Company Intake funnels.
- All previous ATechSpot pages, functions, legal content, accessibility routes and assets.
- Dashboard-compatible `_worker.js` and Wrangler deployment configuration.

## Deploy this version

Upload the V25 ZIP directly to the production deployment of the Cloudflare Pages project connected to `www.atechspot.com`, or extract it and run `DEPLOY-V25-WINDOWS.bat`.
