DAILY RHYTHM

An achievable 80-minute work block.

10 min · Protect service

Check deadlines, buyer questions, returns and unresolved issues.

25 min · Fulfill orders

Locate, inspect, measure and pack. Obtain the carrier acceptance scan.

35 min · Improve inventory

Prepare verified listings or repair existing photographs, details and margins.

10 min · Record results

Log work completed, costs and tomorrow’s first task.

Add time whenever order volume requires it. Fulfillment deadlines take priority over listing targets.
