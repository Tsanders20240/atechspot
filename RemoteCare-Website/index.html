SECURITY & CONSENT

Your device. Your credentials. Your decision.

Remote support should never require blind trust. RemoteCare is designed around explicit permission and minimum necessary access.

  • Do not send passwords or one-time authentication codes.
  • Do not send Social Security numbers or full payment-card data.
  • Enter credentials yourself when a legitimate login is required.
  • Do not accept an unexpected remote-support connection.
  • You can end an authorized remote session at any time.
  • Back up important data before substantial troubleshooting when practical.

SECURE SUPPORT REQUEST

Tell us what needs fixing.

A request is free to submit. It does not create a paid session, guarantee a repair or reserve an appointment. We review your information and reply with the next appropriate step.

Do not include: passwords, verification codes, Social Security numbers, full card numbers, banking credentials or private files.

By submitting, you agree to be contacted about this request. See Privacy, Terms and Payments & Billing.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what to expect before you request help.

How much does RemoteCare cost?

There is no charge to submit a request. Pricing depends on the issue and approved scope. We confirm the applicable fee before paid work begins.

Do you connect to my computer immediately?

No. A request starts review only. Any remote connection requires separate authorization after the scope and price are agreed.

What if the problem cannot be fixed remotely?

We explain known limitations and recommend a practical referral or next step when hands-on repair, hardware replacement or specialist service is more appropriate.

Can a business use RemoteCare?

Yes, for approved technology issues. The person requesting access must have authority to permit work on company-managed devices, accounts and systems.
