## Legal design boundary
V22 does not include Apple, Microsoft, Lucid, or other third-party logos, images, fonts, source code, proprietary copy, or cloned page layouts. It uses general UX concepts that are common across premium websites—large editorial typography, modular cards, guided discovery, clean navigation, progressive disclosure, responsive motion, and clear conversion paths—implemented in A+ Techucation's own visual language and code.
