Accessibility

Technology should be usable by more people.

ATechSpot is committed to improving access to its digital information and services.

Our accessibility goal

ATechSpot, operated by A+ Techucation LLC, works to make ATechSpot.com usable with a broad range of browsers, devices and assistive technologies. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as a reference for ongoing improvement.

Features built into this site

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus indicators
  • Skip-to-content links
  • Semantic headings and labeled forms
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Responsive layouts for smaller screens and magnification
  • Reduced-motion support where animation is used
  • Live status messages for supported forms

Need an accommodation?

If a page, document, form or service creates an accessibility barrier, submit a request through our contact system or call (713) 396-2993. Describe the content or task you need help accessing. Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As pages, services and integrations change, we review issues and prioritize fixes based on impact and feasibility.
