Accessibility

Technology should be usable by more people.

ATechSpot is committed to improving access to its digital information, forms and services across common devices and assistive technologies.

Our accessibility goal

ATechSpot, operated by A+ Techucation LLC, works to make ATechSpot.com usable with a broad range of browsers, devices and assistive technologies. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as a reference for ongoing improvement and treat accessibility as part of product quality rather than a one-time checklist.

Features built into this site

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus indicators
  • Skip-to-content links
  • Semantic headings and labeled forms
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Responsive layouts for smaller screens and magnification
  • Reduced-motion support where animation is used
  • Live status messages for supported forms

Need an accommodation?

If a page, document, form or service creates an accessibility barrier, submit a request through our contact system or call (713) 396-2993. Describe the page or task you need help accessing and the type of problem you encountered. Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information.

How we respond

Accessibility reports are reviewed alongside security, privacy, compatibility and reliability considerations. Depending on the issue, we may correct the page or control, provide information through another reasonable format, improve labels or navigation, or adjust a future release. Third-party tools can sometimes introduce limitations outside our direct control; when that happens, we evaluate practical alternatives and document the next step.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Websites, integrations, browsers and assistive technologies change over time, so a page that works well today can require improvement later. Feedback helps us prioritize barriers that prevent a visitor from understanding information, submitting a request, completing a payment or reaching support. Our goal is to keep the core customer journey understandable and operable while the ATechSpot ecosystem continues to grow.
