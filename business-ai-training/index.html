What to know before booking

Do I need to purchase AI software first?

No. We begin with accessible tools suitable for your workflow. Paid accounts or integrations are optional and quoted separately.

How many people are included?

The $199 pilot includes the owner and up to two employees, with one 90-minute guided training session.

Will AI send messages automatically?

No. The introductory pilot focuses on drafts a person reviews before use. Automated sending and access to sensitive systems are not included.

When do I pay?

After discovery and agreement on the pilot scope and terms, ATechSpot supplies a secure payment link for the approved service.
