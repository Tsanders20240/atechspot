Common questions

Clear expectations before you buy.

Can you guarantee eBay Top Rated?

No. eBay awards Top Rated status based on its own account history, sales volume and performance metrics. We can audit the gaps and help improve the workflows that affect those metrics.

Can you sell commercially on Vinted for me?

No. For U.S. Vinted standard accounts, this service is limited to organizing and optimizing compliant personal-closet listings. We will not build a commercial resale operation on Vinted.

Do you need my marketplace password?

Not for the initial audit. We begin with public pages, screenshots/exports you choose to provide, and non-sensitive seller metrics. Any later account access must be separately authorized and use the least access necessary.

What if I sell on several marketplaces?

Choose the Multi-Channel Pro System or request a custom scope. We can build an inventory and cross-listing workflow designed to reduce duplicate work and overselling.
