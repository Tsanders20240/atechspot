THE 4-CHECK COMPARISON

Compare products like a smart shopper.

Most decisions get clearer when you compare the same four things in the same order.

01

Fit

Does it work with the devices, software, space and habits you already have?

02

Daily experience

Which features will you actually notice every day?

03

Total cost

Include accessories, subscriptions, storage, repairs and replacement cycles.

04

Longevity

Look at support, repairability, ports, capacity and upgrade limits.

SAVE THIS RULE

If two products solve your problem equally well, buy the simpler one.
