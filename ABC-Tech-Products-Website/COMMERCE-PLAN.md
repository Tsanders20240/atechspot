# ABC Tech Products Commerce Plan

## Public architecture

Front-end brand:
- A+ Techucation
- ABC Tech Products
- ABC of Technology ecosystem connection

Production/fulfillment:
- Tapstitch stays behind the scenes.

Recommended commerce layer:
- Shopify Basic.

Virtual try-on:
- Use a Shopify virtual try-on app with a free plan first.
- Current candidate categories include Try-On.AI, FitRum, Torziva, Veyo, and similar free-plan apparel try-on apps in the Shopify App Store.
- Pick the provider only after the Shopify store is authorized and the first live Tapstitch apparel products are published.

## Flow

Customer -> abctechproducts.atechspot.com -> Shopify product/cart/checkout -> Tapstitch production + fulfillment.

The shopper should not be sent into the private Tapstitch account dashboard.

## No-fake-product rule

Do not present a product as purchasable until the exact Tapstitch item has:
1. been designed,
2. published to Shopify,
3. synced to Tapstitch,
4. had variants/prices reviewed,
5. passed a test order sync.

## Virtual try-on privacy

The custom landing page may preview a user-selected photo locally in the browser. It must not claim true AI garment rendering until a connected VTO provider is active on published product pages.
