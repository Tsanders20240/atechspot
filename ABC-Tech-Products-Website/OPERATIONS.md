# ABC Tech Products — Commerce Operations

## Property role

ABC Tech Products is Website 5 of the public ATechSpot ecosystem and the commerce/lifestyle expression of A+ Techucation.

## Tapstitch operating model

Tapstitch is the production-and-fulfillment focal point for apparel operations.

Internal dashboard:
https://www.tapstitch.com/account/dashboard

The private dashboard is for administrators only and must not be linked from the public storefront.

Use Tapstitch as the source of truth for:
- apparel blank selection
- garment variants, sizes and colors
- artwork placement
- product creation
- production configuration
- fulfillment status
- connected-store product publishing
- order-production operations

## Customer-facing brand

Customers should see:
- A+ Techucation
- ABC Tech Products
- ABC of Technology
- connected ATechSpot ecosystem properties

Customers should not be routed into the private Tapstitch account dashboard.

## Collection architecture

1. ABC Tech Kids

2. Future Mode
   - Gen Z teens
   - AI, coding, gaming, creator culture
   - fashion-forward streetwear

3. A+ Tech Core
   - Gen Z adults
   - creators, entrepreneurs, educators
   - cleaner technology-lifestyle apparel

## Publishing rule

Do not represent a product, price, size, color or shipping estimate as live on the public site until that exact product is configured and publishable in the connected commerce workflow.

## Brand rule

A+ Techucation is the master brand. ABC Tech Products is the commerce/lifestyle property. ABC of Technology supplies the children's learning/IP connection. ATechSpot Creator supplies the creator-business connection.
