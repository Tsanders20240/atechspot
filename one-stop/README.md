# ATechSpot one-stop client hub

Public route: https://atechspot.com/one-stop/

## Client path
1. Search a goal or choose a service in alphabetical order.
2. Select one or more of the 22 catalog properties. Filters do not discard selections.
3. Submit goals, desired outcome, budget, timing and contact details.
4. ATechSpot reviews availability and sends a written scope and quote.
5. After approval, the customer opens Cash App $atechucation or PayPal ATECHUCATION.
6. Jason verifies funds in his account and confirms scheduling/onboarding.

## Delivery and operating limits
- The existing `/api/intake` sends submitted information to `aplustechucation@gmail.com` through the existing Resend configuration.
- Selected properties and services are included in the request. Acknowledgment delivery is reported only when confirmed by the endpoint.
- Cash App and PayPal links are external payment destinations; they do not supply automatic payment verification, fulfillment or appointment confirmation.
- Preview and content-only properties remain labeled. Selection requests review; it does not guarantee availability or establish a purchasable service.
- Catalog comes from the existing 22-property directory. Some destination links have not been independently reverified in this change.
- The correct current site is `atechspot.com`; `atechspot.atechspot.com` is not configured.
- No Lee Mobile Mechanic content or payment accounts are included.

## Follow-up checklist
- Verify both payment recipients on the business owner's phone.
- Submit one real intake and confirm receipt in the business inbox.
- Send a written quote showing deliverables, amount due, delivery date and payment reference.
- Verify cleared payment; provide a receipt and start only the agreed work.
