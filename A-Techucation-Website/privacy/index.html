Privacy Notice

Effective September 2026

A+ Techucation is a specialized education property operated by A+ Techucation LLC within the ATechSpot ecosystem. This notice explains how information may be handled when you use the learning property, membership features, support routes or secure checkout.

Information you provide

When you enroll, request support, ask for billing help or use member features, we or our service providers may receive information such as your name, email address, organization, learning goal, membership plan, support request and other details you choose to provide.

Payments and Stripe

Applicable membership checkout is processed by Stripe. Payment-card information is entered directly into Stripe Checkout rather than an ordinary A+ Techucation website form. A+ Techucation may receive information Stripe makes available to merchants, such as customer and transaction identifiers, payment status, billing contact details, payment-method type and limited card details such as brand and last four digits. Do not send full card numbers, CVV codes, online-banking passwords or one-time authentication codes through learning tools, support forms, email or chat. Stripe’s own privacy notice and terms govern Stripe’s processing.

Learning and technical data

If member tools, learning progress, quizzes or AI study features are enabled, the service may process information needed to provide those features, such as progress, responses, settings, browser/device information or technical logs. Separate notices may apply when a third-party learning platform is used.

How information is used

  • Provide learning access and support
  • Manage membership, billing, cancellation, refunds and customer service
  • Maintain progress and member settings where those features are enabled
  • Operate, secure and improve the learning experience
  • Maintain accounting and business records and meet legal obligations
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents

AI study tools

Do not submit passwords, Social Security numbers, full financial-account numbers, payment-card details, medical records or other unnecessary sensitive information into AI study tools. AI-generated responses may be incomplete or incorrect and should be verified when the topic matters.

Service providers

Hosting, email, analytics, payment, membership, customer-support or learning-platform providers may process information as necessary to provide their services. Their own terms and privacy notices may also apply.

Retention

We retain information as reasonably needed for learning access, support, accounting, tax records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security and legal obligations. Some billing records may need to be retained after a membership ends.

Your choices

For a privacy request, use the secure Legal contact route. For billing or cancellation, use Billing & Cancellation.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the learning platform, membership systems or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
