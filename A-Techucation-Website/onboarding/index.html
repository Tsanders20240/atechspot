Member Onboarding

Welcome to your next learning step.

This page starts the onboarding sequence after an enrollment or checkout flow returns to A+ Techucation.

1. Confirm enrollment

Your membership is activated only after the payment/enrollment record is confirmed. A checkout session ID in the URL by itself is not proof of payment.

2. Confirm your email

Use the email address associated with enrollment for membership instructions and support.

3. Take your placement assessment

Return to the interactive placement assessment to identify the best first learning path.

4. Choose one starting path

Start with the highest-priority skill instead of trying to complete every school at once.

5. Open your member dashboard

Once membership access is provisioned, use your dashboard to see the next lesson, progress, practice activity and available AI study support.
