Membership Billing

Clear pricing. Simple cancellation.

A+ Techucation memberships are billed securely through Stripe.

Current monthly membership prices

  • A+ Starter — $19/month
  • A+ Pro — $39/month
  • A+ Business — $79/month

Each membership is a recurring monthly subscription. The checkout page shows the exact amount and identifies the charge as recurring before you submit payment. Membership renews at the same billing interval until canceled or changed under the applicable terms.

Secure checkout

Membership checkout is hosted by Stripe. Payment-card information is entered directly into Stripe Checkout rather than an ordinary A+ Techucation website form. A+ Techucation does not ask you to email a full card number, CVV, online-banking password or one-time authentication code.

Cancel future renewals

You may cancel future membership renewals before the next billing date. There is no A+ Techucation cancellation fee for stopping future renewals. Unless applicable law or a specific written offer requires otherwise, access normally continues through the already-paid billing period and no further renewal is charged after cancellation is processed.

Request Membership Cancellation →

When Stripe self-service billing access is enabled for your account, you may also use the secure billing-management link provided in your Stripe customer experience or A+ Techucation account.

Refunds and billing errors

Monthly membership fees are generally for access during the purchased billing period and are not automatically prorated after the period begins. Duplicate charges, unauthorized charges, technical billing errors and any refund rights required by law are handled separately. Contact Billing promptly if something is wrong.

Plan changes

Upgrades or downgrades may change your recurring amount and access level. Any new amount and timing should be shown before the change is confirmed. A plan change is not effective merely because someone requests information about another tier.

Taxes

Applicable taxes may be collected where required. Tax treatment can vary by customer location and the nature of the digital learning service.

Need help?

Use the secure Billing route for subscription, cancellation, payment or refund questions. Do not send full payment-card or bank credentials.
