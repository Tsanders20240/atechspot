Know what happens before you invest.

What happens after the free assessment?

You receive an opportunity preview on-screen. If the signals justify deeper analysis, you can request the $997 Executive Growth Review. No detailed custom roadmap is generated by the free form.

What does the $997 Executive Growth Review buy?

It buys professional analysis and prioritization across brand, website, conversion, search, sales, follow-up, AI, automation, revenue model and scalability. Detailed work begins after payment confirmation.

Is implementation included in the $997 review?

No. Implementation is a separate engagement. If implementation is appropriate, A+ Techucation sends a written scope with deliverables, timeline and pricing for approval.

No. A+ Techucation can improve systems, execution and decision quality, but business outcomes depend on many factors outside any consultant's control.

Yes. The Revenue Fix option is designed for a clearly defined priority. Larger builds are recommended only when multiple connected systems truly need attention.

How does GrowthCare work?

GrowthCare is recurring optimization and maintenance after launch. Exact coverage is confirmed in writing so both sides know what is included, what is out of scope and how priorities are handled.
