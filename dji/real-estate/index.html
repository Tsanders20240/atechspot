REAL ESTATE DRONE GUIDE • UPDATED SEP 11, 2026

Best DJI drone for real estate photography.

Choose the aircraft around the property work—not the spec sheet. This guide separates a portable listing-content setup from a higher-end paid production setup, then shows the shot and delivery workflow that turns aerial footage into a repeatable service.

Quick answer:

ATechSpot recommends DJI Mini 5 Pro for solo agents and creator-first teams that prioritize a compact kit. Choose DJI Mavic 4 Pro when you are selling professional aerial media and the larger multi-camera platform can earn its keep.

03 / REAL ESTATE SHOT PLAN

Capture what helps a buyer understand the property.

Aerial footage works best when it adds orientation, scale and context—not when every shot is simply higher.

Front elevation + approach

Show the home, driveway and immediate setting cleanly before moving into dramatic angles.

45° corner perspective

Use a moderate oblique angle to show depth, lot relationship and architectural form.

Backyard / amenity context

Pool, acreage, water, detached structures and outdoor-living areas often benefit from aerial context.

Neighborhood orientation

Show proximity to relevant surroundings without implying exact distances or legal boundaries unless verified.

Slow reveal

Use a deliberate movement from foreground to property rather than aggressive FPV-style motion for standard listings.

Vertical social cut

Capture or frame material that can become a clean 9:16 reel in addition to the MLS/media package.

05 / U.S. FLIGHT CHECK

Commercial work means operational discipline.

ATechSpot is not a law firm or aviation authority. Use the FAA as the source of truth for U.S. requirements.

Part 107

Paid real-estate photography is a business/non-recreational use case. In the U.S., commercial small-drone operations generally fall under FAA Part 107 or another applicable FAA authorization pathway.

  • Keep the aircraft within visual line of sight unless an applicable authorization says otherwise.
  • Operating limits, people/vehicle rules and weather requirements still apply.
  • Registration and Remote ID requirements may apply based on the operation and aircraft.

FAA Part 107 overview ↗

Airspace authorization

Real-estate work near controlled airspace can require authorization. FAA resources describe LAANC and other authorization processes for Part 107 pilots.

Do not promise a client a flight before checking the location, airspace, site conditions and any applicable local restrictions.

07 / FAQ

Real-estate drone buying questions.

What DJI drone should a real estate agent buy?

For a compact listing-content setup, ATechSpot currently prefers Mini 5 Pro. If the agent is hiring out the work rather than flying personally, the more important decision is the media provider's portfolio, licensing and workflow.

When is Mavic 4 Pro worth it?

When premium commercial deliverables and multi-perspective coverage are part of what clients pay for. A larger flagship platform is harder to justify if the service only needs a few basic exterior aerials.

Do I need a drone for every listing?

No. Aerials are most valuable when they add context: lot size, acreage, water, neighborhood orientation, roof/grounds, architectural layout or nearby amenities.
