05 / BEFORE YOU BUY

Check the whole system.

The aircraft is only part of the purchase. A useful setup includes the controller, batteries, charging, storage and the legal/operational requirements for your use case.

Controller & bundle

Confirm exactly which controller is included and whether the bundle matches the way you plan to fly.

Batteries & charging

Paid work often needs more than one flight battery plus a charging plan that matches the job schedule.

Storage & media

Check internal storage, supported media and your file-transfer workflow before the first client job.

Replacement parts

Propellers, guards and other field-replaceable items reduce downtime when the aircraft becomes a business tool.

Insurance & client terms

For paid work, consider liability coverage, written scope, weather policy and deliverable terms appropriate to the job.

Specialized requirements

Survey-grade mapping, thermal inspection, engineering and other specialized work may require different sensors, workflows or enterprise equipment.

06 / U.S. FLIGHT CHECK

Buy the drone. Know the rules.

ATechSpot is not the FAA or a law firm. U.S. requirements depend on how, where and why you fly, so verify the current FAA rules for your operation.

Recreational flying

FAA recreational rules include the TRUST safety test, visual-line-of-sight requirements, airspace rules and registration requirements based on aircraft weight and operation type.

The FAA says drones at or below 0.55 lb (250 g) flown only under the limited recreational exception generally do not require registration; Part 107 operations have separate registration requirements.

FAA recreational flyer guidance ↗

Business / Part 107

The FAA's Part 107 framework covers many commercial small-drone operations. Part 107 pilots must register each drone they operate, and applicable airspace/operating requirements still apply.

FAA Part 107 overview ↗

Remote ID

The FAA says drones that are required to be registered or are registered must comply with Remote ID, unless operating under an applicable exception such as within a FRIA.

FAA Remote ID guidance ↗

Do not assume “small” means unrestricted

Weight can affect registration treatment for purely recreational operations, but it does not erase airspace, safety, operational or Part 107 requirements. Check the exact mission before flying.

08 / FAQ

Quick buying answers.

Which DJI drone should a beginner buy?

ATechSpot's current first-drone pick is Neo 2 for easy personal capture, follow shots and a guarded compact design.

Which DJI drone is best for travel creators?

Mini 5 Pro is the current ATechSpot travel-creator pick when portability, stronger imaging and true vertical shooting are priorities.

Which DJI drone is best for professional work?

Mavic 4 Pro is the current flagship recommendation when paid deliverables can benefit from the 4/3 Hasselblad main camera, dual tele perspectives and broader production flexibility.

Should I buy Avata 360 instead of Mini 5 Pro or Mavic 4 Pro?

Only when immersive FPV/360 capture is central to your work. It is a specialized creative format rather than a routine linear upgrade from a conventional camera drone.
