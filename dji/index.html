06 / BEFORE YOU FLY

Make the buying decision with the operation in mind.

ATechSpot is not a law firm or aviation authority. The FAA remains the source of truth for U.S. operating requirements, registration, certification and airspace rules.

U.S. flight-check basics

  • Registration requirements depend on how and what you fly.
  • Remote ID requirements may apply to registered aircraft.
  • Business/non-recreational operations generally require the applicable FAA pathway.
  • Airspace, line-of-sight and operating-location restrictions can change the legality of a flight.

07 / QUICK ANSWERS

Know what you're buying—and who you're buying it from.

Are these actual photos of the named drones?

Yes. The six product cards use real third-party photographs of the exact named DJI models. The files are sourced from Wikimedia Commons under CC0 or CC BY-SA licenses, with attribution displayed beneath each image where required.

Is ATechSpot DJI an official DJI website?

No. ATechSpot DJI is an independent buying-guide and decision-tool property within the ATechSpot ecosystem. It is not owned or operated by DJI.

Why doesn't ATechSpot show a fixed product price?

Because price, promotions, inventory, bundles, shipping and regional availability can change quickly. The external seller page is the transaction source of truth.

Which model is the ATechSpot starting point for real estate?

Air 3S is our default starting recommendation for a balanced real-estate/business workflow. Mavic 4 Pro is the step-up when higher-end paid deliverables justify flagship capability.

Which model is the ATechSpot starting point for a beginner?

Neo 2 for low-friction personal capture. Flip for a more traditional creator-drone experience with a guarded design. Mini 5 Pro when a compact travel kit and stronger imaging become the priority.
