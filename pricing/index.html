WHAT THE PRICE COVERS

Know what is included—and what is separate.

Clear boundaries protect both the customer and the project.

Typically included in the approved scope

  • Discovery and requirements specific to the purchased engagement
  • Documented deliverables and implementation responsibilities
  • Agreed design, development, automation, configuration or consulting work
  • Reasonable testing and handoff for the agreed deliverables
  • Communication and status updates appropriate to the engagement

Not automatically included

  • Work outside the approved scope
  • Third-party subscriptions, hosting, domains or licenses
  • Advertising spend, marketplace fees or app-store fees
  • Premium plugins, external APIs or vendor charges
  • Additional revisions, features or integrations not listed in the agreement

THIRD-PARTY COST NOTICE

External services may have their own fees.

Hosting, domains, payment processing, software subscriptions, licenses, premium plugins, APIs, advertising, app-store fees and other third-party services are not included unless the written proposal or checkout specifically says they are. Any known required third-party costs are identified before implementation when reasonably possible.

BUYER PROTECTION

Clarity before commitment.

ATechSpot does not begin unspecified paid implementation work simply because a visitor submits a form.

Before paid implementation

  • The business problem or desired outcome is identified
  • The service or project scope is defined
  • Pricing and payment terms are disclosed
  • Key third-party dependencies are identified
  • The customer chooses whether to proceed

For recurring services

  • The recurring amount and billing frequency are disclosed
  • The included recurring scope is documented
  • Future renewals require the disclosed recurring authorization
  • A cancellation route is provided
  • Stopping future renewals does not create an undisclosed cancellation fee

For complete transaction terms, see Terms of Use & Service and Payments & Billing.

PRICING FAQ

Questions before you buy.

Are the published prices final quotes?

Prices labeled “from” or “starting at” are planning anchors. Final scope, deliverables, dependencies, third-party costs, schedule and pricing are confirmed in writing before custom implementation.

How is technical support priced?

We review the issue first and confirm the applicable scope and fee before a paid support session begins.

When does paid work begin?

Custom implementation begins only after the applicable scope and payment terms are accepted. Submitting a form or assessment alone does not start paid work.

Are third-party tools included?

Not automatically. Hosting, domains, subscriptions, licenses, premium plugins, APIs, advertising and other external fees are separate unless the written scope specifically includes them.

Can a project cost more than $2,500?

Yes. $2,500 is the published starting point for project builds. The final price depends on the approved scope, complexity, integrations, deliverables and third-party requirements.

How does GrowthCare billing work?

GrowthCare starts from $750 per month. The recurring amount, billing frequency, included scope and cancellation method are disclosed before recurring service is activated.

What if I am not sure which service I need?

Start with the free Growth Assessment or tell us the problem through the project intake. We will recommend an appropriate next step rather than requiring a larger service automatically.
