Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how A+ Techucation LLC, operating the ATechSpot brand (“ATechSpot,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use ATechSpot.com and applicable ATechSpot services. Separate ecosystem properties may publish additional notices when their data practices differ.

Information you provide

We may receive information you submit through forms, assessments, service requests, checkout or communications, such as your name, business email, phone number, organization, website, project needs, budget range, desired outcomes, billing contact information and other information you choose to provide.

Payments and Stripe

ATechSpot uses Stripe for applicable checkout, subscription and payment processing. When you enter payment-card or eligible bank-payment information in Stripe Checkout, that sensitive payment information is submitted directly to Stripe rather than to an ordinary ATechSpot website form. ATechSpot may receive information Stripe makes available to merchants, such as customer and transaction identifiers, payment status, billing name and address, email, payment-method type and limited card details such as brand and last four digits. We do not need or ask you to send a full card number, CVV, online-banking password or one-time authentication code through ATechSpot forms, email or chat. Stripe’s own privacy notice and terms govern Stripe’s processing.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting, security, analytics and payment providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring pages, timestamps, checkout events and security signals.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and evaluate project fit
  • Provide requested services, learning access and support
  • Process, reconcile and support payments, subscriptions, invoices, refunds and disputes
  • Operate, secure and improve the website
  • Measure marketing and website performance
  • Maintain accounting and business records and comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents

Service providers and sharing

We may use service providers that support hosting, email delivery, analytics, scheduling, payments, customer management, learning systems or security. Providers receive information only as needed for the services they perform and are governed by their own legal obligations and contracts. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety or systems.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, service delivery, accounting, business records, dispute resolution, security and legal obligations. We use administrative and technical safeguards intended to reduce risk, but no internet system is guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of certain information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention needs, through the ATechSpot Legal contact route. Billing records may need to be retained for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes even after a service ends.

Third-party links and changes

External websites and payment processors are governed by their own practices. We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
