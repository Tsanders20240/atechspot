Commerce & Payment Terms

Effective September 2026 · Applies to applicable Stripe checkout transactions for ATechSpot and A+ Techucation.

Merchant identity

ATechSpot and A+ Techucation are operated by A+ Techucation LLC. When a Stripe checkout page identifies A+ Techucation LLC as the merchant, A+ Techucation LLC is the business charging the disclosed amount for the applicable ATechSpot service or A+ Techucation membership.

Before you pay

The checkout flow and the page that sends you to checkout are intended to disclose the material payment terms, including the product or service, amount, whether the charge is one time or recurring, and the applicable cancellation information. Do not complete checkout unless the price, billing frequency and offer are clear to you.

A+ Techucation recurring memberships

Standard A+ Techucation memberships are currently A+ Starter at $19/month, A+ Pro at $39/month and A+ Business at $79/month. A membership renews monthly until canceled. By completing subscription checkout, you authorize the disclosed monthly charge to recur until cancellation. You may cancel future renewals before the next billing date through the billing method provided with the service or the A+ Techucation Billing & Cancellation page. A+ Techucation does not charge a cancellation fee merely for stopping future renewals. Unless applicable law or a specific offer states otherwise, access normally continues through the already-paid period.

ATechSpot one-time and recurring services

Defined one-time offers, such as the Executive Growth Review, are charged once at the amount shown at checkout. Custom Project Builds require written scope and payment terms before implementation. Recurring ATechSpot services such as GrowthCare are activated only after scope, recurring amount and service expectations are confirmed in writing; they renew at the disclosed interval until canceled.

Refunds and billing errors

Refund eligibility depends on the purchased offer, whether professional work has begun, the applicable service agreement and applicable law. Membership fees are generally for access during the purchased billing period and are not automatically prorated after the period begins. Duplicate charges, unauthorized charges and technical billing errors should be reported promptly. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Applicable taxes may be collected where required. Tax treatment varies by location and the nature of the service or digital product.
