Children's Privacy & Parent Notice

Effective September 2026

ABC's of Technology is a child-directed educational property created for young learners and families. A+ Techucation LLC operates the property and is committed to minimizing data collection from children and giving parents clear information about how the site works.

Children should not submit personal information

Children should not send or enter their full name, home or school address, email address, telephone number, photographs, videos, voice recordings, precise location, account credentials, payment information, Social Security number, government identification number, or other personal information through ABC's of Technology.

Parents and guardians should supervise a child's use of the site and use adult contact information when communicating with us.

No child account is required

The core learning activities on ABC's of Technology are designed to be used without creating a child account. Interactive learning features such as vocabulary, spelling, games, coloring and similar educational activities should not require a child to provide personal information to participate.

Information processed automatically

Like most websites, hosting, security and infrastructure providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser or device type, request timestamps, security signals and pages requested. ABC's of Technology is not intended to use children's personal information for behavioral or targeted advertising.

Because third-party code, embedded media, plug-ins and analytics can create additional data collection obligations on a child-directed service, those services must be configured and reviewed for child-directed use before they are enabled. Where privacy-enhanced or non-personalized modes are available, they should be used.

Embedded media and third-party links

The site may link to or display content from third parties, including video platforms, booksellers and product sellers. Adult purchases occur on the third party's site, not through a child-facing ABC's of Technology checkout form. Third-party websites are governed by their own privacy practices and terms.

Parents may choose not to activate embedded media or follow external links. We do not authorize third parties to use information collected through the child-directed experience for targeted advertising to children.

Purchases are for adults

Book and product purchase links are intended for parents, guardians and other adults. Children should not enter payment-card, billing, shipping or other purchase information on this site.

Parental consent

ABC's of Technology is designed not to require children to submit personal information for ordinary learning activities. If a future feature would collect personal information from a child under 13 in a way that requires consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and Rule (COPPA), that feature must not collect the information until the required notice is provided to a parent and verifiable parental consent is obtained, unless a specific legal exception applies.

Parent rights and requests

A parent or legal guardian may contact us to ask about information associated with their child, request access where applicable, request deletion where applicable, or ask that further collection or use stop. We may take reasonable steps to verify that the person making the request is the child's parent or legal guardian before disclosing or deleting information.

Parent and privacy requests may be submitted through the ATechSpot Legal contact route or by email to legal@atechspot.com. General support is available at support@atechspot.com or (713) 396-2993.

Data minimization, retention and security

Any personal information collected in connection with a legally permitted child-directed feature should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for that feature, protected with reasonable safeguards, and retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the specific purpose for which it was collected or as otherwise required by law. It should not be retained indefinitely.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the site's features, service providers or legal requirements change. Material changes involving collection, use or disclosure of children's personal information will be handled in accordance with applicable law, including any required parent notice and consent.

Operator

ABC's of Technology is operated by A+ Techucation LLC as part of the ATechSpot ecosystem. Parent privacy inquiries should be directed to the contact methods above.
